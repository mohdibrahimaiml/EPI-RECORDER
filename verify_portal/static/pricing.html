Frequently asked questions

Why is the CLI free forever?

Because cryptographic evidence is only trustworthy if it's trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn't be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed: verify, modify, distribute without us. That's the whole point.

What does Hosted give me?

Convenience. Upload a .epi file through your browser and get a verification report without installing anything. 10,000 checks/month, up to 10 API keys, remote SCITT anchoring, and founder email support. Everything the CLI does offline remains free whether you subscribe or not.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

Full Hosted access. No charges for 7 days. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed. Your plan reverts to Free at the end of the trial if you don't continue.

Why isn't there a team or enterprise price listed?

Self-serve checkout today is Open Source + Hosted only. Backend plan keys are free, hosted (aliases: pro/starter), team, and enterprise. Team/Enterprise are promoted via invoice + operator set-plan or design-partner engagement — not a fixed public SKU until dedicated infrastructure can back an SLA. See known limitations.

What is notarization and why is it free?

EPI seals evidence with RFC 3161 timestamps from public time authorities and optionally anchors proofs into the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps. This proves when evidence was created, not just what it contains, without depending on EPI Labs infrastructure. We ship it free in the CLI because timestamping shouldn't require a subscription.

Is the hosted backend production-grade?

It runs on shared infrastructure. Suitable for individual practitioners. May experience occasional cold starts. Offline CLI verify works regardless. Organizations needing dedicated infrastructure should contact us for an Enterprise engagement. See status page and known limitations.
