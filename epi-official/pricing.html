Design partner inquiry

Straight answers

Why is verification free forever?

Cryptographic evidence only works if it’s trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn’t be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed. That’s the spine of the company.

What do I get in a $1,500 sprint?

Five focused days with the founder: one agent workflow instrumented end-to-end, sealed .epi artifacts your team can re-verify offline, and a short findings report. It is priced against unblocking enterprise “prove the agent” deals — not against billable hours.

Where is Hosted / Pro monthly pricing?

Removed from this page on purpose. We will not post a monthly number for infrastructure that is not ready to back a promise. Free offline verify stays free. Optional shared hosted checks may still appear on Account for existing free-tier usage — they are not the product we sell as a paid SKU today.

What is a design partner?

Someone who needs org keys, hosted receipts, or retention before the control plane is generally available. You get founding pricing, direct roadmap input, and a pilot from $5K. Capacity: a handful of partners, not a self-serve plan grid.
