Frequently asked questions

Can I use the open source version in production?

Yes. EPI Recorder is MIT licensed. The CLI seals, verifies, multi-signs Annex sections, and can emit Annex PDF reports offline. Paid tiers add hosted verification scale, remote SCITT, API keys, and support — not a different evidence format.

What do I actually get if I pay for Pro?

A higher monthly hosted verification quota (10,000), up to 10 API keys, remote SCITT registration, and founder email support. Everything for recording and verifying .epi files offline remains free. The hosted backend currently runs on shared infrastructure — check the status page for uptime before subscribing.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

You get full access to the Pro plan. No charges for the first 7 days. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed. Your plan downgrades to Free at trial end if you don't subscribe.

How does Enterprise work?

Enterprise is a services engagement, not a pre-packaged SaaS tier. We scope dedicated infrastructure, onboarding, and support after a discovery call. SLA terms are project-specific and written into the engagement, never sold as a generic guarantee before we understand what you need.

Is the hosted backend production-grade?

The hosted verify service currently runs on shared free-tier infrastructure that may experience cold starts. It's suitable for individual practitioners testing the hosted path. Enterprise customers get dedicated infrastructure. Offline CLI verify (epi verify) has no dependencies and works regardless.

Do I need a paid tier for EU AI Act Annex IV technical artifacts?

No. The open-source CLI produces Annex IV artifacts with signatures, hash chains, multi-signer approvals, and optional local SCITT receipts. That is technical evidence tooling — not legal advice or a notified-body certification.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account page or by contacting support. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
