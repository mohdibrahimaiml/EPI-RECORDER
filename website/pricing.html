Frequently asked questions

Why is the CLI free forever?

Because cryptographic evidence is only trustworthy if it's trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn't be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed: verify, modify, distribute without us. That's the whole point.

What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

Both give you 10,000 hosted checks/month. Pro adds remote SCITT anchoring, up to 10 API keys (vs 1), and founder email support. Starter is $1/month for the hosted basics. The offline CLI is the same on both -- it's free either way.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

Full access to the plan you selected. No charges for the first 7 days. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed.

What is notarization and why is it free?

EPI seals evidence with RFC 3161 timestamps from public time authorities and optionally anchors proofs into the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps. This proves when evidence was created, not just what it contains, without depending on EPI Labs infrastructure. We ship it free in the CLI because timestamping shouldn't require a subscription.

Is the hosted backend production-grade?

It runs on shared infrastructure. Suitable for individual practitioners. May experience occasional cold starts. Offline CLI verify works regardless. Organizations needing dedicated infrastructure should contact us for an Enterprise engagement.

How does Enterprise / consulting work?

You email us, we scope what you need -- compliance mapping, integration, dedicated infrastructure, custom development. Priced as a bounded engagement after we understand the scope. No pre-packaged SLA, no generic tier.
