Frequently asked questions

Why is the CLI free forever?

Because the value of cryptographic evidence infrastructure is that it's trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn't be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed so you can verify, modify, and distribute it without us. That's the whole point.

What does Pro actually give me?

Convenience. Upload a .epi file through your browser and get a verification report without installing anything. 10,000 checks/month, up to 10 API keys for CI pipelines, and your evidence is anchored into a remote transparency log. Everything the CLI does offline remains free whether you subscribe or not.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

Full Pro access. No charges for 7 days. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed. Your plan reverts to Free at the end of the trial if you don't continue.

Is the hosted backend production-grade?

It runs on shared infrastructure today — suitable for individual practitioners. It may experience occasional cold starts. Offline CLI verify (epi verify) has no dependencies on any server and works regardless. Organizations needing dedicated infrastructure should contact us for an Enterprise engagement.

Do I need a paid tier for EU AI Act compliance?

No. The free open-source CLI produces Annex IV technical documentation with cryptographic signatures, hash chains, and multi-signer approvals. That's the evidence tooling. Legal interpretation and regulatory submission are separate from the tool — we offer compliance-readiness consulting as a paid service to help you navigate that gap.

How does Enterprise / consulting work?

It's a conversation, not a shopping cart. You email us, we scope what you need — compliance mapping, integration, dedicated infrastructure, custom development — and price it as a bounded engagement. No pre-packaged SLA, no generic tier. You pay for real work, not server uptime promises we can't yet make.
